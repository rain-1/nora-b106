# Dragon P3.2 requirements

- Use a centred USB-C USB 2.0 receptacle with both plug orientations wired.
- CC1/CC2 each require their own 5.1 kΩ pull-down; SBU pins remain unconnected.
- Restore four M2 holes with at least 10 mm antenna-to-hardware courtyard clearance.
- Use 40.5 × 49.5 mm, 1.6 mm FR4, keeping within a 50 mm square size tier.
- Retain twelve GPIOs, P1's J2/J3 mapping and the unchanged shared firmware.
- Minimum assembly stays module plus two local bypass capacitors; USB stays optional.
- Retain the optional reset button, dragon/credit, RF keepouts and ground reference.
- Update BOM, paste artwork, separate drill files, checks and current Gerber ZIP.
- No manufacturer purchase or hardware-tested claim.
